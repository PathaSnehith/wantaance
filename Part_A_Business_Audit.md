### 1. Executive Summary & Production Volume
Sharma Precision Engineering manufactures custom sheet-metal enclosures, electrical panel cabinets, laser-cut mounting plates, and machine guards primarily in Cold Rolled Close Annealed (CRCA) mild steel and AISI 304 Stainless Steel. 
- **Workforce:** 14 full-time workers (Laser operator, 2 press-brake operators, 4 welders/fitters, 2 helpers, 1 CAD draftsman, 1 accountant/munshi, 3 external tempo/field staff).
- **Order Volume:** 45 to 60 custom job orders per month. Average job value ranges from ₹18,000 to ₹2,50,000. Total monthly throughput: ~16–22 tonnes of processed sheet metal.

---

### 2. Order Arrival & Intake Channels
Orders arrive through three unstructured channels:
1. **WhatsApp (75% of volume):** Customers send 2D DXF CAD files or rough PDF sketches directly to Rakesh's personal WhatsApp, accompanied by voice notes: *"Bhaiya 10 piece CRCA 2mm mein urgently kaat do."*
2. **Direct Shop-floor Walk-ins (20%):** Local panel builders bring physical damaged samples or cardboard templates to replicate.
3. **Phone Calls (5%):** Repeat industrial clients place verbal re-orders based on past job names (*"Pichli baar wala 500 kVA canopy frame 4 set bhej do"*).

### 4. Tracking Infrastructure Today
- **The "Bahi-Khata" Register:** A large hardcover ruled ledger on Rakesh's desk where orders are logged chronologically. When an order is completed, Rakesh draws a single blue line across the row.
- **Magnetic Job Board:** Yellow carbon-copy job slips clipped to a steel sheet near the laser machine. Slips frequently get smudged with cutting oil or blown away by cooling fans.
- **WhatsApp Chat Threads:** The true real-time communication spine. Customer revisions, approvals, and tempo driver location pins live in separate 1-on-1 chats.
- **Tally Prime:** Resides on a desktop in an air-conditioned cabin, used exclusively by the part-time accountant for final GST billing and bank entry. It has zero link to floor work-in-progress (WIP).

---

### 5. Primary Failure Mode & Real Cost
- **The Core Failure:** **Outside Job-Work (Powder Coating) Black Hole & Drawing Revision Desynchronization.**
  1. Once parts leave the factory gate for outside powder coating, all operational visibility vanishes. If the vendor delays chemical treatment or paints the wrong RAL shade, Sharma only discovers it when the customer is already screaming for delivery.
  2. Customers frequently send revised CAD drawings (*"Rev-2 final hai"*) on WhatsApp after cutting has already begun on Rev-1.
- **Quantifiable Cost:**
  - **Direct Material Scrap:** ₹25,000 to ₹40,000 per month in scrapped laser-cut sheets due to obsolete drawing versions.
  - **Repainting / Re-work & Penalties:** ₹15,000/month in two-way tempo freight and strip-down recoating charges.
  - **Working Capital Lockup:** ~₹2.5 Lakhs in delayed client receivables because a 95% finished enclosure is missing 2 brackets stuck at the plater.
