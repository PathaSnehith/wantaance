1. Executive Summary & Throughput

Sharma Precision Engineering is an industrial sheet metal job-shop fabricating custom electrical panel enclosures, machine covers, and laser-cut stainless steel mounting plates in CRCA and SS 304. They employ 14 full-time workers and process 45 to 60 custom job orders per month (~16 to 22 tonnes of raw steel). Order values range from ₹18,000 to ₹2,50,000.

2. Order Arrival Channels

  • WhatsApp (75%): Customers ping 2D CAD files (DXF/PDF) directly to Rakesh's WhatsApp with audio notes: "Bhaiya 10 piece 2mm sheet mein urgently kaat do."
  • Shop-floor Walk-ins (20%): Local contractors bring physical damaged sheet enclosures or cardboard templates for replication.
  • Direct Phone Calls (5%): Verbal re-orders based on past job names ("Pichli baar wala 500 kVA canopy frame 4 set repeat kar do").

4. Primary Failure Mode & Cost

The Core Fracture: Outside Job-Work (Powder Coating) Black Hole & Drawing Revision Desynchronization

Once parts leave the gate for outside powder coating, all operational visibility evaporates. If the vendor coats the wrong RAL shade, the owner only discovers it when the customer is screaming for delivery. Furthermore, customers routinely send revised CAD drawings ("Rev-2 final hai") on WhatsApp after laser cutting on Rev-1 has already finished.

Quantifiable Cost: ₹25,000–₹40,000/mo in scrapped steel; ₹15,000/mo in rework freight; and ~₹2.5 Lakhs in locked receivables on 95% complete jobs missing 2 delayed brackets.
